    On the timesheet

    1. Invoice lines at the top: one line per day or week, with Copy buttons for your Revolut Business invoice. They don't print.
    2. Edit an entry to change its date, times or task. It's marked “adjusted” with the original time, also on the printed sheet.
    3. Exclude anything you won't charge for. It stays on the sheet, crossed out, and isn't counted.
    4. + Add entry for time you didn't track. Added entries are marked “added”.
    5. Press Print, then Save as PDF, for a PDF to send.
