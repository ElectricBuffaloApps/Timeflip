    Make a timesheet

    Pick a client and a month.

    On the timesheet

    1. Create invoice at the top makes a numbered invoice from the sheet (set up your details in Settings first). Or use Invoice lines, with Copy buttons, to paste into another invoicing app.
    2. Edit an entry to change its date, times or task. It's marked “adjusted” with the original time, also on the printed sheet.
    3. Exclude anything you won't charge for. It stays on the sheet, crossed out, and isn't counted.
    4. + Add entry for time you didn't track. Added entries are marked “added”.
    5. Save PDF downloads the timesheet as a PDF to send (it lands in your Downloads folder).

    Limits

    Mac notification at each alert level

    Hours. Leave a box empty for no limit. Tick Bill as allowance on a task's weekly limit to get a monthly maximum (the weekly hours shared across the month's working days).     Bank holidays (England and Wales) aren't counted.
